================================================================================
BRICK SIEGE - HOW TO PLAY
================================================================================

You defend a LEGO fortress from waves of brick creatures, or you play the other
side and lead the assault. Both sides are playable and both can win, from the
same six maps and the same four themes.

Everything you build is made of bricks. Nothing is downloaded - every tower,
creature, tree and mountain is generated when the game loads.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE TWO SIDES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Choose your side on the title screen. You can switch sides from the pause menu
at any time.

  DEFENDER   You own the fortress. Build towers on the empty studs, pay off the
             25 waves, and stop the lane from ever reaching the gate.

  ATTACKER   The fortress belongs to the defender. You have no towers of your
             own - you drop creatures at the gates and walk them down the lane
             until they break in.

The win condition is different on each side, so read the objective line at the
top of the screen if you are ever unsure which one you are playing.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CAMERA
  Drag with the left mouse button, or with one finger, to pan.
  Right-drag also pans.
  WASD or the arrow keys also pan.
  Scroll wheel, or pinch with two fingers, to zoom.
  On a touchscreen, twist two fingers to turn the view, and drag two fingers
  up or down to change the pitch. The turn is limited either side of straight
  on, so the board never flips over.

BUILDING (defender)
  Click or tap a tower in the shop, then click an empty stud to place it.
  Tapping a placed tower selects it, which highlights anything you can merge.
  Sell with the SELL button on the selection panel, or with Delete / Backspace.
  Selling refunds 60% of everything poured into the tower.

  Keys 1-8 select a tower:
    1 Brick Shooter    2 Mortar        3 Freezer      4 Tesla Coil
    5 Sprayer          6 Long Shot     7 Cluster      8 Beacon

DEPLOYING (attacker)
  Click or tap a creature in the palette, then click one of the marked tiles.
  Press the same number again, or tap the palette again, to cancel.

  Keys 1-9 select a creature:
    1 Stud Slime       2 Bone Builder  3 Crumble Zombie
    4 Bow Brick        5 Ghost Brick   6 Gun Brick
    7 Brick Demon      8 Rocket Brick  9 The Overlord

  Q cycles through the creatures you can afford.

OTHER KEYS
  P            pause / unpause
  SPACE        defender: send the next wave early, earning 3 bricks for every
               second of build clock you skip
               menu: start the run
  ESCAPE       cancel the current selection, deployment or merge;
               if nothing is selected, opens the pause menu
  C            merge the selected tower with a matching neighbour
  X            switch between 1x and 2x game speed
  H            open or close the codex
  M            mute or unmute


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DEFENDER - HOW TO PLAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

YOUR GOAL
  Survive all 25 waves with fortress health above zero. The fortress has 20 HP.
  Each creature that reaches the gate removes its "leak" value, so letting a
  Stud Slime through costs 1, a Brick Demon costs 3, and an Overlord costs 10.

MONEY
  You start with 260 bricks.
  You earn more by clearing waves - the payout for each wave is listed in the
  wave preview, and it grows from 40 bricks early on to 600 for the last wave.
  Selling a tower refunds 60% of what you paid for it, so a bad early tower is
  never a disaster.

THE BUILD CLOCK
  You have 30 seconds to place your first tower before wave 1 starts.
  After that you get 18 seconds between waves. Use the clock: a tower placed
  early earns more waves of shooting for the same bricks.
  Press SPACE to call the next wave early. You are paid 3 bricks for every
  second of the clock you skip, so a confident early call is real money.

WHERE YOU CAN BUILD
  Any stud that is not part of a lane and not part of the fortress. Lanes are
  the studded paths the creatures walk; leave them clear or you will block
  your own guns.

MERGING - THE MAIN WAY TO GET STRONG
  Drop a second tower of the same type on top of an existing one and the two
  merge into a stronger single tower.

    MK I  ->  MK II  ->  MK III

  MK II and MK III towers hit far harder, reach further and reload faster.
  MK III is the strongest thing you can build. Merging is free - it costs no
  bricks, only the space to place a matching pair.

COMBINING RANGE
  Two towers merge when they are the same type, the same tier, and within 3
  studs of each other. Matching towers you drop next to each other on purpose
  will merge whether or not you meant them to.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DEFENDER - THE NINE TOWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 BRICK SHOOTER   60 bricks
   The dependable one. Cheap, accurate, no tricks. Range 3.2.
   MK I    8.5 dmg   every 0.60s
   MK II  13.5 dmg   every 0.55s   range 3.6
   MK III   20 dmg   every 0.50s   range 4.1

 MORTAR         110 bricks
   Lobs shells that splash over a wide area. Slow, so it is useless against
   fast swarms on its own. MK III fires two shells at once.
   MK I    26 dmg   every 1.90s   splash 1.1
   MK II   40 dmg   every 1.75s   splash 1.35
   MK III  58 dmg   every 1.60s   splash 1.6, double shell

 FREEZER          95 bricks
   Does very little damage but chills everything it hits, slowing it by up to
   55%. Pair it with anything else and that tower does far more work.
   MK I     7 dmg   every 1.00s   -35% speed for 1.6s
   MK II   11 dmg   every 0.90s   -45% speed for 2.0s
   MK III  16 dmg   every 0.80s   -55% speed for 2.4s

 TESLA COIL      130 bricks
   Arcs between creatures, so one shot can hit several. The damage falls off
   with each jump, but against a tight pack it is the best damage in the game.
   MK I    11 dmg   every 1.15s   chains to 1 extra
   MK II   17 dmg   every 1.00s   chains to 2 extra
   MK III  24 dmg   every 0.90s   chains to 3 extra

 SPRAYER         100 bricks
   Short range, but the mist poisons and keeps working after the shot lands.
   Poison damage is what actually kills - the spray itself only ticks them
   down and sets it up.
   MK I     3 dmg +  6 poison/sec for 3.0s
   MK II    5 dmg + 10 poison/sec for 3.5s
   MK III   7 dmg + 16 poison/sec for 4.0s

 LONG SHOT        95 bricks
   Enormous range and enormous damage, but very slow to reload. Nothing else
   reaches this far, so it covers the back of a long lane on its own.
   MK I    44 dmg   every 2.3s   range 6.4
   MK II   72 dmg   every 2.1s   range 7.0
   MK III 108 dmg   every 1.9s   range 7.6

 CLUSTER          85 bricks
   Chunky short-range shells that splash and chill a whole group. Weak on a
   single target, devastating against anything bunched up.
   MK I    12 dmg   every 1.4s   splash 1.3, -30% speed
   MK II   19 dmg   every 1.3s   splash 1.5, -35% speed
   MK III  28 dmg   every 1.2s   splash 1.7, -40% speed

 BEACON           70 bricks
   Deals no damage at all. It projects a field that makes nearby towers hit
   harder and fire faster. Put it in the middle of a cluster.
   MK I    +18% damage, +12% speed to towers within 2.6
   MK II   +28% damage, +18% speed to towers within 3.0
   MK III  +40% damage, +25% speed to towers within 3.4


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. ATTACKER - HOW TO PLAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

YOUR GOAL
  Break the fortress before the 300 second clock runs out. The fortress has
  20 HP. Every creature that reaches the gate removes its leak value.

WHERE YOU CAN DEPLOY - LOOK FOR THE ARROWS
  At the start of each lane there is a floating blue arrow pointing down at the
  ground, with a ring under it. Those arrows are your deployment points.

    - The arrows are always visible on your turn. You do not need to select a
      creature first to see them.
    - Select a creature and the arrows brighten and bob faster.
    - The marked area covers the first 3 tiles of each lane, just inside the
      gate.
    - FOUR PATHS has four gates, so you get four arrows, one on each side.

  You can only drop creatures on a marked tile. This is deliberate: a creature
  has to walk the whole lane and fight through the defence, so dropping one
  right next to the fortress would skip the entire puzzle.

MONEY
  You start with 160 bricks and earn 5 bricks every second, so the clock is
  also your income. Every second you spend thinking is a creature you cannot
  afford.

REWARDS FOR BREAKING THINGS
  Every defender tower you destroy pays you 45 bricks. If it was a merged
  MK II or MK III tower, you get 70. So you are paid for dismantling the
  defence as well as for reaching the gate.

SIEGE
  Your creatures deal triple damage to defender towers. That is what lets you
  chew through a merged MK III line that would otherwise be untouchable, and
  it is why picking fights with towers is often worth more than rushing the
  gate.

6. ATTACKER - THE NINE CREATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  #  CREATURE          COST    HP     SPEED   LEAK
  -  ----------------  ------  -----  ------  ----
  1  Stud Slime         20      42    1.05     1
  2  Bone Builder       35      34    1.55     1
  3  Crumble Zombie     45      98    0.72     2
  4  Bow Brick          70      46    1.85     1
  5  Ghost Brick        55      58    1.38     1
  6  Gun Brick          85      78    1.50     1
  7  Brick Demon        80     215    0.62     3
  8  Rocket Brick      110     165    1.05     2
  9  The Overlord      150    1500    0.48    10

  Speed is in tiles per second. Leak is fortress damage if it gets home.

  Stud Slime     Cheap and endless. Filler, but filler is what pays for the
                 next wave of units.
  Bone Builder   Very fast and very cheap. Good for swamping a gap.
  Crumble Zombie Slow, sturdy, and hits the fortress for 2.
  Bow Brick      The fastest thing in the game, and it shoots your towers from
                 3.4 tiles out. Kill it early or it thins your defence for you.
  Ghost Brick    Quick and slippery.
  Gun Brick      Ranged. Can shoot the towers it passes.
  Brick Demon    A real wall of HP, and costs 3 fortress damage.
  Rocket Brick   Heavy ranged hitter, and costs 2 fortress damage.
  The Overlord   Enormous HP and 10 fortress damage in one hit. Save it for
                 when the gate is already weak, or use it as a battering ram
                 against a merged tower line.

  On the defender side the wave health rises 8.5% every wave, and wave speed
  creeps up too. A creature that walked through wave 1 will not survive wave
  20, so you have to keep upgrading the pressure, not just repeating yourself.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. THE SIX MAPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  SPIRAL       Long sweeping lanes that curl around the keep. Long Shot and
               Mortar shine here because every lane passes the same ground
               twice.
  CROSSFIRE    Arms shoot in from every side and meet at the gate. You need
               coverage on all four approaches or one side is a free walk.
  SERPENT      Saw-tooth runs with tight switchbacks. Slow guns struggle; the
               Freezer keeps things slowed long enough for the others.
  RINGS        Concentric circuits. Every tower covers two approaches, so
               range matters more than raw damage.
  MAZE         Organic switchbacks, no two runs alike. The hardest map to
               plan and the easiest to get surprised on.
  FOUR PATHS    Four roads crossing at the fortress. Hold every arm, or break
               in from any of them.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. THE FOUR THEMES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Pick a theme on the title screen. It applies instantly and can be changed
  mid-run from the pause menu.

  FOREST     Grass and woodland. Broadleaf trees, pines, fallen logs,
             undergrowth, a cut stump and a mossy boulder.
  DESERT     Warm sand. Cacti, palms, obelisks, a stepped pyramid and a
             sphinx.
  ARCTIC     Snow and ice. Frosted pines, ice spires, icebergs, three polar
             bears and an igloo.
  VOLCANO    Ash and lava. Charred trunks, obsidian shards, and a volcano
             with a glowing crater that lights up the scene.

  A theme only changes the scenery. The baseplate, the lanes and the fortress
  are identical on every theme, so no theme makes a map easier or harder.

  The scenery is scattered in loose clumps with clearings between them, so it
  grows like real woodland rather than standing in rings around the board.
  Nothing is ever placed on the baseplate, so it never hides a lane or a
  buildable stud.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. STRATEGY NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DEFENDING
  - Cover the early lanes cheaply, then merge. Four MK I shooters merged into
    two MK IIs is far stronger than four MK I shooters.
  - The Freezer multiplies every other tower. Chill the lane first, then let
    the damage land.
  - The Beacon is only worth bricks next to other towers. On its own it does
    nothing at all.
  - Look at what is actually coming. A wave preview that lists 17 Zombies
    wants a Freezer and a Cluster, not a Long Shot.
  - If a lane is not defended, the creatures will go around it. Walk each lane
    with your finger before you commit your money.
  - Selling at 60% means a badly placed early tower is cheap to fix. Do not
    be precious about your first placement.

ATTACKING
  - Spread across gates. The defender has to cover all of them, and on
    CROSSFIRE and FOUR PATHS that is a lot of ground.
  - The 45 brick bounty is real money. Two Gun Brick kills early can pay for
    the Overlord.
  - Overlords cost 10 fortress damage each. Three of them end the run, but
    they are slow, so a Freezer-heavy defence will hold them a long time.
  - Watch the clock. At 5 bricks a second, a 300 second run is worth roughly
    1500 bricks of spending plus whatever you loot. Do not sit on it.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. END OF RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  When a run ends you get a summary: waves survived or fortress damage dealt,
  bricks earned and spent, towers merged, and creatures lost. From there you
  can restart the same map, go back to the menu and change map, map or theme,
  or play the other side.

  Settings, chosen side, map and theme are all remembered between sessions.
